### 2. Exterior Painting
- slug `exterior-painting`
- H1: "Exterior Painting in Maryland"
- Meta title: "Exterior Painting in Maryland | KrasPro LLC"
- Meta description: "Exterior house, barn, vinyl siding and metal roof painting in Maryland. Lead-safe prep, power washing, stripping and window reglazing. Free estimates."
- Sub-services (cards): Houses; Barns; Metal roofs and chimneys; Vinyl siding painting; Power wash and prep painting; Stripping all paints; Window reglazing; Lead paint removal.
- Benefits seeds: prep-first approach so paint lasts; protection from Maryland humidity and freeze-thaw cycles; stronger curb appeal and home value; lead-safe practices on older homes; tidy, protected job site.
- Process: 1) Inspect and document surfaces, 2) Wash, scrape, strip and repair, 3) Prime and caulk, 4) Apply finish coats and complete a walkthrough.
- Q&A:
  1. Q: How often should I repaint the exterior of my house in Maryland? A: Well-prepared exterior paint on siding commonly lasts roughly 7 to 10 years, and sun-exposed walls and trim may need attention sooner. Maryland's humid summers and freeze-thaw winters are hard on coatings, and surface prep matters more than paint brand.
  2. Q: Do you have to remove the old paint before repainting? A: Not always. Sound paint can usually be scraped, sanded, primed and painted over. If paint is peeling, blistering or built up in failing layers, it should be stripped back to a sound surface first, otherwise the new coat will fail too.
  3. Q: Is lead paint a concern for older Maryland homes? A: Homes built before 1978 may contain lead-based paint. Under the EPA's Renovation, Repair and Painting (RRP) rule, contractors who disturb paint in these homes must be EPA Lead-Safe Certified and follow lead-safe work practices, and Maryland has additional rules for pre-1978 rental properties. Ask any contractor for their certification before work begins.
  4. Q: Can vinyl siding be painted? A: Yes, with proper cleaning and the right product. Use paint formulated for vinyl and a color close in lightness to the original, because dark colors can absorb heat and warp vinyl.
  5. Q: Can you paint barns, metal roofs and chimneys? A: Yes. Metal surfaces need thorough cleaning, rust treatment and a metal-appropriate primer before finish coats. Barns usually need extra washing and scraping on rough-sawn wood. We assess the condition first and tell you what prep it needs.

  4. Q: What is exterior capping and is it worth it? A: Capping wraps wood trim such as fascia and window trim in aluminum so it no longer needs repainting and is better protected from moisture. It works best over sound wood, so rotted areas are repaired first.
